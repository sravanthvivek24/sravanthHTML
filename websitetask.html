📢INTRODUCTION

Virat Kohli (born November 5, 1988) is a legendary Indian international cricketer and widely regarded as one of the greatest all-format batsmen in the history of the sport. Known for his incredible consistency and chasing prowess, he has amassed over 85 international centuries across all formats. 

🏏CAREER HIGHLIGHTS 🏏

  • Virat Kohli made his international debut in 2008 and quickly established himself as one of the most promising young batsmen in the country.
  • He became the fastest player to score 10,000 runs in ODI cricket.
  • Kohli is the only player to have scored over 7,000 runs in T20 internationals.
  • He has been the captain of the Indian cricket team in all formats.

PERSONAL LIFE

Virat Kohli is married to Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma. The couple got married in December 2017 and have a daughter named Vamika, born in January 2021. Kohli is known for his fitness and dedication to the sport, often sharing glimpses of his training routine on social media.

🏆 AWARDS AND RECOGNITION

Virat Kohli has received numerous awards and accolades throughout his career, including the Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy for ICC Cricketer of the Year multiple times, the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna award, and the Padma Shri, one of India's highest civilian honors.

CONCLUSION

Virat Kohli's impact on Indian cricket is immeasurable, and his legacy as one of the greatest cricketers of all time is firmly established. His dedication, passion, and relentless pursuit of excellence continue to inspire millions of cricket fans around the world.
